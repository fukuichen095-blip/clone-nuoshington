About Us

Nuo Shington (Hangzhou) Precision Machinery Co.,Ltd

For over 20 years, NUO SHINGTON has grown from a specialist manual and electric hoist manufacturer into a comprehensive material handling solutions provider. Today, our product range covers electric hoists, warehouse pallet trucks, stackers, and a full line of forklifts — all manufactured at our 50,000 m² facility in Hangzhou, China.

  • With products exported to 100+ countries across Europe, Southeast Asia, and the Americas, we combine factory-direct pricing with the reliability of CE, EMARK, and RoHS certifications.
