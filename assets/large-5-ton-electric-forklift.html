Products Description

Electric forklifts have become increasingly popular in recent years, and their structure and features differ from traditional gas-powered forklifts.

Electric forklifts have an electric motor instead of an internal combustion engine, which makes them quieter and more environmentally friendly. The battery is typically located under the seat or in the rear of the forklift and can be easily removed and replaced.

The forklift's controls and gauges are digital, with a display screen located in the instrument panel. This allows for greater accuracy in controlling the lift and making adjustments.

The forklift's mast, which supports the lift carriage, is typically made of steel and can be adjusted to different heights. The carriage itself contains the forks, which can be adjusted in width to accommodate different sizes of pallets or loads.

The forklift's steering and maneuverability are enhanced through features like a compact design and tight turning radius. This makes electric forklifts ideal for use in narrow aisles or confined spaces.

The unique structure and features of electric forklifts make them a popular and efficient option for material handling in a wide range of industries.

 

 

The drive system uses a horizontal fan-shaped drive axle arranged in parallel with a large transmission ratio. The battery is located at the bottom of the chassis; the truck has good stability.

An integrally forged integrated steering axle with a shock mitigation system with excellent driving comfort and prolonged service life.

The ratchet-type parking brake allows the truck to stay on the slope of 15% without safety concerns.
