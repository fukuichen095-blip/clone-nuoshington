Description

Product Description

Forward visibility has been improved 10%. The wide mast, with lift chain spacing, makes the view forward less cluttered to enhance safety and improve productivity.

Safe inspection. The engine hood has an automatic lock. This fail-safe stopper prevents the hood from closing unexpectedly, ensuring safe inspection under the hood.

The optimal gearshift pattern is selectable Trucks with an automatic transmission come with a three-month selection switch; you can select the automatic transmission gearshift pattern according to your working condition with the lift of the switch.

Durable hydraulics. Oil-filled cylinders The lift cylinder piston rods are filled with hydraulic oil to improve the durability of the lift cylinders.

Safe starting. Neutral safety mechanism. The neutral safety mechanism prevents the engine from being started unless the directional control lever is in the neutral position. This prevents the truck from moving abruptly when the starter switch is turned on in both hydraulic and manual transmission trucks.

Outstanding visibility from the rear, high-mount, non-stop lamps. The stop lamps are installed at the rear on the top of the overhead guard to provide improved visibility to nearby workers or other vehicles behind the truck, thus offering added safety in the workplace.

Adapt to all kinds of rough road surfaces.

Large-size deep-pattern off-road vacuum tire with excellent off-road capability and shock absorption performance.

Generous ground clearance and optimized
dimensions provide excellent maneuvering and stability under the terrain.

Fully opening up the bonnet reveals the convenient access to mechanical components for simplified maintenance.

With an original integrated suspension chassis design, adopting four anti-vibration pads below, it effectively reduces vibration and improves operator's comfort.

Wide-view mast
The wide-view mast design provides better vision and increases working stability and safety, which is more suitable for outdoor working and easy entrance of the pallet.

 

 

 

Cooling system
The cooling system is designed with upgraded technology, reliability, and a better heat dissipation effect. Satisfied with continuous working condition at a temperature of +43 degrees.
