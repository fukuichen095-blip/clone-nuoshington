Description

A 2-ton pallet handling forklift is a powerful piece of equipment designed to move and handle heavy-duty pallets weighing up to 2 tons. It is an efficient, reliable machine ideal for use in warehouses, distribution centers, and manufacturing plants.


A 2-ton pallet-handling forklift is an excellent investment for any business that needs to move heavy items efficiently and safely. Its high lifting capacity, maneuverability, safety features, and ease of use make it a valuable asset to any warehouse or manufacturing facility. We are committed to helping our customers run their business smoothly by providing fast and efficient service.
We have an extensive product range of material handling equipment:
Internal combustion counterbalanced forklifts from 1.8 tons to 45 tons
(diesel and gasoline).


1-ton to 16-ton electric counterbalanced forklift
(Available with lead-acid or lithium-ion batteries).
Warehousing equipment includes 13-meter reach forklifts, ride-on pallet trucks, pallet stackers, micro-electric pallet trucks, and the latest AGVs (automatic guided vehicles).
