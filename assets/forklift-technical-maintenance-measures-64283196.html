Forklift Technical Maintenance Measures

Jul 19, 2026

1. Routine maintenance: after each shift.
2. First-level technical maintenance: after accumulatively working 100 hours, one-shift working system is equivalent to 2 weeks.
3. For secondary technical maintenance: after accumulatively working 500 hours, one shift is equivalent to one quarter.
