Description
Products Description

The diesel-powered forklift has a fully floating overhead guard that lowers the center of gravity and improves product stability and residual lifting capacity. Second, our durable chassis features a full web guard structure that protects the engine bay from dust, water, and grime. More importantly, our fuselage is very strong and will not be easily damaged. And we also have high-performance tires, which greatly extend the service life of the tires. Finally, our products are very versatile and add nice productivity benefits to workers.

 

By continually improving the specifications and level of our products, we can guarantee the stability of our diesel-powered forklift. We welcome you to visit our company and factory. The company pays attention to the role of people, the cultivation of quality, the development of talents, being people-oriented, and quality first. The company will build a good career platform for employees; managers will be promoted from the grassroots, and employees and the company will grow together.

 

Feature

The best feature of a diesel-powered forklift is that we have an LCD display that allows the operator to monitor all critical systems during operation to help reduce costs and the risk of excessive downtime. Secondly, our products can mechanize loading and unloading, saving a lot of labor, reducing labor intensity, and shortening loading and stacking time. More critically, this can improve work efficiency, speed up material and vehicle turnover, greatly improve warehouse utilization, promote the development of warehouses to multi-level shelves and high-bay warehouses, improve operational safety, and reduce commodity damage. Finally, our products have a good stability performance.

 

 

Application

The diesel-powered forklift is an important means of modern logistics transportation, suitable for yards, airports, stations, ports, warehouses, factories, workshops, and distribution centers. It can reach the cabin, load cargo, and unload and handle containers.

(1) Transmission: enhanced intelligent automatic shift transmission, smooth shift without impact, prolonging the service life of the transmission.
(2) Transaxle: forklift truck special heavy-duty transaxle, axle housing strength is high, bearing capacity is large, anti-pollution ability is strong, good heat dissipation, braking is stable, reliable, has good performance and cost performance.
(3) Hydraulic system: adopts load-sensing hydraulic technology and reliable performance.
(4) Braking system: The pneumatic cap oil technology is adopted for the clamp disc braking system, which is safe, reliable, and convenient to maintain.
(5) High-strength structural parts: the frame structure with high-strength plate and box design is more durable.
(6) Suspension vibration isolation design between the power transmission system and the frame and door frame is adopted to reduce vibration and noise.
